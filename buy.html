How It Works

1
Buy on Weekdays

Place your buy order from Monday to Friday. The market price determines your purchase price.

2
Earn Daily Rates

Your earnings depend on the day you buy: Monday = 5%, Tuesday = 4%, Wednesday = 3%, Thursday = 2%, Friday = 1%.

3
Company Fee

A 1% fee is deducted from your earnings. Your net earnings are the rate minus 1%.

4
Sell on Weekends

On Saturday and Sunday, you can sell your shares and receive your initial investment plus your net earnings.

5
Physical Stock

Buying early gives the company time to find customers and transport physical stock for delivery.
